# Quality review

## Evaluate without fake precision
Use pass / needs revision / not checked with concrete evidence. No invented quality percentages or guaranteed virality. Check these separately:
1. Concept: the visual idea is specific to the message.
2. Comprehension: a viewer can identify the main point and focal subject.
3. Composition: hierarchy, spacing, crop and contrast work at intended size.
4. Assets: the focal subject has an authored silhouette, meaningful detail and usable changed states; geometry, cutout edges, perspective/contact, lighting where used, style, resolution and licensing are coherent. Inspect both close crop and full composition; a detailed specification is not an inspected asset.
5. Motion: the subject performs an explanatory action; staging, easing, overlap and settle feel coherent at normal speed. Reject a film built entirely from identical entrances.
6. Continuity: transitions preserve subject, shape, direction or focus where useful, or mark a deliberate contrast. No accidental snaps or unrelated scene swaps.
7. Rhythm: density and tempo vary; reading and listening get adequate time.
8. Technical: fonts, assets, browser errors, seek/restart, controls and audio work.
9. Evidence: visual and narration are supported; illustrations are labeled.
10. Audio: real narration exists for the explainer default, pronunciation is checked when listening is possible, music/SFX support intelligibility, and voice/visuals share transport. A script, missing file or browser TTS promise does not pass.
11. Design specificity: distinctive subject/medium and contrasting compositions express this topic; typography and palette alone do not establish visual appeal.

## Review procedure
Before full build inspect opening, explanation and payoff keyframes. Then inspect a representative motion passage. Fix major composition problems before polishing tiny effects.

Include an asset-craft pass within that review: does the main element hold up as artwork, relate clearly to the typography and environment, and perform its planned action? If the whole direction depends on an unresolved generic hero element, redesign it before multiplying scenes. Do not mistake more texture, bevel, glow or detail for improvement.

For final review sample every beat at entry, readable hold and exit; inspect transitions and first/last frame. If feasible replay short intervals to detect pops, jitter, collisions, pacing and audio drift that still frames miss. Capture portrait layout at actual viewport. Verify font readiness and offline dependencies rather than trusting screenshot fallback fonts.

Make a short defect list with time, element, observed problem, likely fix and severity. Fix critical missing content, incorrect facts, broken playback, clipping and illegibility first. Then address the dominant creative weakness. Normally perform up to two focused refinement passes; if a material issue remains, continue or clearly report it rather than hiding it behind “polished”. Stop optional refinements once evidence supports delivery.

## User revision
Change only requested scope, preserve approved content. If “kurang keren” is the sole feedback, diagnose and propose one concrete improvement direction, then execute reversible visual changes. Ask for a reference only when it would materially resolve uncertainty.

## Report honestly
Say what was inspected (files, frames, playback, audio, metadata), what passed, what remains blocked. Gallery screenshots are references, not tests of the newly generated work. Do not claim automatic checks establish aesthetic excellence. For untested runtime mark status clearly.

## Release decision

- **Final:** requested content and sound are present, and the performed checks are reported honestly. Do not imply cross-browser certification or professional quality without evidence.
- **Preview — narration pending:** the concept/animatic is useful but required voice cannot yet be produced. State the exact dependency; do not ask the user to approve a hidden downgrade.
- **Preview — playback unverified:** browser playback could not be inspected. Syntax checks and font metrics may still be reported, but motion, synchronization and visual quality remain unchecked.

Statuses may coexist. Do not fabricate a pass, and do not keep adding optional effects/tests when a clearly identified external limitation is the remaining issue.

For “stiff, silent, unattractive,” inspect choreography, audio capability/defaults, and art direction as separate causes. Repair workflow and resources rather than appending “make it premium” to the prompt. When revising only the skill, keep the previous rendered project intact until a new production is requested.
