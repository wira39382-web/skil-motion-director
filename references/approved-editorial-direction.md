# Approved editorial direction

Use this default for Wira's informative, technology, business and current-affairs motion unless the current brief requests another identity. Treat the user's selection on 22 October 2026 as approval of this visual direction, not proof of final technical/audio quality.

## Concrete benchmark

Open `assets/benchmarks/editorial-ai-45s.html`: the selected revision 04 of the AI investment film. It preserves the first film's editorial energy with modernized geometry. Use it as a visual/pacing reference, not a universal script or factual dataset. Its statistics have explicit dates and must be rechecked for a new story. Do not inherit its unused code or placeholder audio status into a new final production.

## Preserve

- Editorial typography, strong hierarchy and brisk changes of composition. Use concise headlines and let objects explain the mechanism.
- Paper `#f4f1e9`, navy `#132239`, cobalt `#2f4eea`, orange `#ed8654`. Alternate light, dark and cobalt scenes to distinguish arguments; do not flash these colours rapidly.
- Barlow Condensed for headlines and Manrope for supporting text, with bundled/authorized equivalents if needed.
- Modern layered isometric or dimensional vector objects: crisp geometry, restrained material gradients, purposeful edge highlights, coherent shadows and recognizable moving parts.
- Variation across close object, wider infrastructure relationship, bold data, access barrier, everyday device and network payoff. Choose equivalent subjects for the new topic; do not paste a chip into every film.
- Active assembly, path drawing, transfers, changes of scale and state, and typography tied to the subject. Overlap supporting actions while preserving a clear focal action.

## Pacing reference, not a quota

The selected 45-second benchmark has seven narrative beats and nine main compositions. The infrastructure passage changes between chip, data centre and electricity about every 2.16 seconds. Directional transitions take about 0.24 seconds; text/object arrivals generally take 0.4–0.7 seconds. Data and dense statements receive longer reading holds. Adapt these times to measured narration and comprehension, rather than forcing every shot to last two seconds or accelerating the whole film.

A request for smoother motion means refine acceleration, overlap and continuity while retaining this energy. Do not simply slow down every shot. Avoid prolonged static holds with only floating dots as a substitute for subject action. Maintain meaningful variety; visual volume is not a target by itself.

## Avoid regression

Do not reinterpret “more modern / more premium / more interesting” as a switch to pastel cartoon machinery, toy gears, cute characters, or a uniformly dark static infographic. Those directions were less preferred in this project. They remain available when explicitly requested for another brief.

When the user asks for more interactive elements, first clarify through the context whether they mean elements interacting within the animation. Do not replace a self-playing film with click-to-open cards or a lesson interface. Make object-to-object interaction primary; add viewer interaction only when it serves the request. Keep review controls outside the clean cinematic frame.

Preserve the chosen direction across revisions. Improve the relevant objects, compositions and choreography without replacing the visual family. Current explicit user instructions and supplied brand assets override this default.

## Audio and evidence remain separate gates

Music/SFX are not narration. Supply an actual recorded Indonesian voice track for a final explainer, align visuals with it, and retain captions. Browser speech may only be a clearly labelled optional demo; it is not a measured, reliably exportable narration master.
